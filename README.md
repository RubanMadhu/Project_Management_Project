# 🌱 Green Campus Energy Optimization & Smart Power Management System

## 📌 About the Project

This is a group project focused on improving energy efficiency and sustainability on university campuses.

The project explores how renewable energy and smart technologies can help reduce electricity consumption, lower energy costs, and protect the environment.

The project is documented in an approximately 3,000-word academic report.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Reduce electricity consumption by a targeted 25% in the first year.
- Introduce energy-efficient LED lighting and motion sensors.
- Explore solar-powered streetlights and rooftop solar panels.
- Reduce electricity costs and energy wastage.
- Promote sustainable energy use on campus.

## ⚡ Proposed Solutions

- **Energy-Efficient Lighting:** Use LED lights to reduce electricity consumption.
- **Motion Sensors:** Automatically control lighting in selected areas.
- **Solar Energy:** Use solar panels to generate renewable electricity.
- **Smart Energy Monitoring:** Track energy consumption and identify potential savings.

## 📊 Project Management

The report covers:

- Project planning and scheduling.
- Budget estimation and financial analysis.
- Stakeholder analysis and team responsibilities.
- Risk assessment and mitigation.
- Project tracking and evaluation.

## 💰 Estimated Budget

- **Initial investment:** Approximately €1,190,944.
- **Estimated annual electricity savings:** Approximately €566,498.
- **Estimated payback period:** 2.1 years.

*These figures are preliminary estimates based on the assumptions in the report.*

## 🌍 Expected Benefits

- Reduced electricity wastage.
- Lower energy costs.
- Increased use of renewable energy.
- Improved environmental sustainability.
- More efficient campus energy management.
